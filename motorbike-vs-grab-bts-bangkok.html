Motorbike Rental vs Grab vs BTS: Cheapest Way Around Bangkok

Should you rent a scooter or just use Grab and the Skytrain? It depends on how much you plan to move around. Here's an honest comparison from a shop that rents bikes but also tells people when they don't need one.

Renting a scooter

Our daily rates start from ฿350 a day, with lower daily rates on a weekly rental — see every bike on our pricing page. Add a few hundred baht of fuel for a busy week. That covers unlimited trips, any time, door to door, including places the BTS doesn't reach.

Best for: people who move around a lot, want to explore beyond the train lines, eat out in different neighbourhoods, or are staying a week or more.

Grab and taxis

Grab cars and taxis are comfortable and air-conditioned, but in Bangkok traffic a cross-town car ride can take a long time, and fares add up when you're making several trips a day. GrabBike (motorbike taxi) is faster and cheaper than a car for short trips. Prices change with demand, so check the app.

Best for: occasional trips, nights out when you're drinking, trips with luggage, or travelling as a family.

BTS and MRT

The Skytrain and subway are fast, cool and cheap, and they skip traffic completely. Fares depend on distance. The catch is coverage: if your hotel and your destinations aren't near a station, you'll still need a taxi or a walk at each end.

Best for: travelling along the train lines at rush hour, and trips to the big malls on Sukhumvit and Siam.

The quick comparison

Cost per trip: scooter lowest once you make several trips a day; BTS low; Grab highest. Rush hour: BTS/MRT wins; scooter second; cars lose. Flexibility: scooter wins. Comfort and rain: Grab car wins. Licence needed: only for the scooter.

Our honest advice

If you'll make two or three trips a day for several days, a scooter is usually the cheapest and most flexible option. If you're mostly going to malls on the BTS line, or you've never ridden before, use the train and Grab — we'd rather you have a good trip than rent a bike you don't need.

Many renters mix all three: scooter for daytime exploring and food, BTS at rush hour, Grab home after a night out.
