Good to know before you book

1 day = 24 hours
Rental days run a full 24 hours from pickup, not calendar days.
Deposit required
฿3,000 refundable deposit for daily/weekly rentals, ฿5,000 for monthly — held for the length of your rental.
Inspect before you ride
We check the bike together at pickup — a quick video is a good idea too.
Full tank out, full tank back
Bikes go out with a full tank — return it the same way.
Late returns are charged
A late fee applies past your agreed return time — message us if you need more time.
Bangkok only
Bikes are for use within Bangkok — let us know if your plans go further, so we can advise.

Questions renters ask us most

Do I need a license?
Yes — a valid motorcycle license or an International Driving Permit with a motorcycle endorsement. ASEAN nationals can use their home-country license directly, no IDP needed. Thai law requires it and it's checked at police stops.
What do I need to book?
Your passport, license, and a refundable deposit — message us at least 1 day ahead to check bike availability, then swing by our Sathorn 19 shop to pick up.
Is insurance included?
Every rental includes basic compulsory (พ.ร.บ.) insurance. It does not cover damage to the bike itself — that's what the deposit is for.
Can you deliver the bike?
No — we don't offer delivery. All rentals are picked up in person at our Sathorn 19 shop.

Not sure which bike fits your trip?

Tell us on WhatsApp what you're planning — city runs, day trips, two riders — and we'll point you to the right model.
